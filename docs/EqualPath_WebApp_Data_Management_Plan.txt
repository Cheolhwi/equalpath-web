Data Management Plan
Temporary care and pickup coordination
Prepared for the EqualPath project team and FIT5120 reviewers. 12 September 2026.
This plan defines the datasets, interpretation rules, preparation, storage, conceptual relationships and analysis required for temporary care and pickup coordination. Collection and controls are planned unless identified as existing reference work.
1 Datasets overview
The named institutional and geographic sources are OpenDOSM, OpenStreetMap, JKM, KPM and Kiddy123. The parent-experience feature also uses a separate supplementary corpus of third-party public reviews. The review corpus is not attributed to those five sources and is not classified as open data.

2.1 Data preparation
Acquire and record
For each permitted source collection, retain source identifier, access route, retrieval time, coverage scope and any source date. Keep partial collections identifiable. Do not claim a complete directory when pagination, authentication or page access was incomplete.
Separate institution branches
Preserve original identity fields where actually acquired, alongside normalised values. Existing JKM imports exclude phone and postal address [R12]; new fields need separate source evidence. Match branches using multiple corroborating signals. Ambiguous matches remain separate rather than inheriting another branch facts.
Reuse the area crosswalk
Use the existing OpenDOSM-backed canonical district labels and explicit JKM aliases. Preserve provider-only or non-one-to-one areas. Do not guess that a locality is an administrative district or silently join a missing area [R12].
Retain individual claims
Store hours, fees, age, transport and registration as separate evidence items. Each retains its source, original wording, date and interpretation. Conflicting sources are visible; the most recently fetched page is not automatically the most authoritative fact.
Normalise with preserved meaning
Represent service hours with applicable weekdays and exceptions, and retain currency and fee basis. AGE below defines the selectable age intervals and partial-overlap rule. Separate no, unknown and contact for information. Reject invalid coordinates rather than repairing them by guesswork.
Prepare review evidence
Separate reviews from editorial and institutional copy. Deduplicate exact or demonstrably repeated items without discarding independently similar experiences. Preserve language and meaning. Topic labels and excerpts must be traceable to original passages; no invented balancing quotes.
Validate before publication
Check identity matches, collection scope, dates, coordinates, fee units and missingness before publication. Existing JKM observations include reused registration identifiers and missing or invalid fields [R12]; this is evidence for branch-review controls, not a completed control test. Failed refreshes retain the last usable version and dates.

2.2 Storage, access and retention

Even without accounts, a place plus service time may reveal a household routine; request and share-card data are therefore treated as context-sensitive rather than automatically anonymous.

2.3 Conceptual data relationships
The conceptual model below defines the relationships needed to keep institutional evidence, local reusable material and shared plans traceable.

Figure 1. Proposed information relationships. Public evidence and user-created private material have different access and retention requirements.
Relationship rules
A source record belongs to a source and a collection event. Several source records may describe one institution branch, while an ambiguous match remains unresolved.
Registration, service, spatial and review evidence refer to the branch without losing their own source identity. Neither a directory review nor an operator note can replace an official registration record.
Local favourites reference branches. Shared card items reference branches and preserve the contact order chosen by the editor; local notes do not transfer automatically.
A rehearsal snapshot refers to a card revision and the fact versions checked. It records the chosen scenario and condition states, allowing a later result to be compared without rewriting history.
Read and edit capabilities are separate secrets associated with the private card. Public source records and search results do not contain or expose these capabilities.
Source traceability record
Each published item needs a stable source reference, branch-match status, original claim or excerpt, normalised interpretation, source date or date precision, retrieval time and a reason for any exclusion or warning. An acquisition timestamp alone is insufficient evidence that a claim is current.

2.4 Data analysis and interpretation
Descriptive evidence
Counts describe the collected sample, not the full platform. A recurring concern needs at least two distinct collected reviews. A topic with fewer than two dated relevant reviews in the recent twelve months is labelled limited recent evidence under story 6.3.3. These are display rules, not statistical proof of reliability or a current institutional problem.
Current request checks
Epic 2 defines supported, conflict and unknown results for age, temporary admission, transport existence, pickup coverage, pickup timing and care end. It compares applicable published pickup windows with the deadline; no route duration is computed. General business hours do not establish a temporary-care window, and actual reception remains a question for the provider.
Conditional scenarios
Epic 8 preserves the actual request and baseline card while changing hypothetical assumptions. Story 8.2 compares baseline and scenario using the same evidence version; story 8.6 retains the same assumptions while comparing newer evidence. Shared limitations and common information gaps are distinct. Neither produces a health score or acceptance probability.
Convenience and costs
Use source-backed location and service factors. This baseline has no route-time calculation; transfer duration and arrival stay unknown when unsupported. Cost comparison preserves the published charging basis and missing extras. A reference total under 3.5.3 requires applicable one-off rates and complete charging rules, as specified by D4.

3 Field definitions and requirement ownership
AGE: The user selects completed age on the service date: Under 1, 1, 2, 3, 4, 5 or 6 years. These mean [0,12), [12,24), [24,36), [36,48), [48,60), [60,72) and [72,84) months respectively; the lower endpoint is included and the upper endpoint excluded. No birth date is requested. This is a product input definition for the current under-seven search scope, not a claim about institutional admission. Unselected age is unknown.
Published service ages retain their original wording and endpoints. A fully included selected interval is supported by published ages; a disjoint interval is a conflict; a partial overlap, unclear endpoint or missing range requires confirmation. For example, age 4 is fully within an explicit 48 to under 72 month range, conflicts with under 48 months, and only partly overlaps 54 to under 72 months. A bare 4 to 6 label needs its endpoint convention checked. Registration category alone never confirms temporary admission.
JKM describes TASKA in terms of children below four years, while KPM defines preschool education for four to six years [R14, R15]. These categories guide evidence lookup, not a promise that a branch accepts a particular child or one-off request. The preserved persona description is historical context; current candidate ages must be checked explicitly.
Shared freshness interpretation
FRESH: Compare source fact versions, preserving both source date and retrieval time. A successful retrieval does not prove a provider updated its facts. A failed refresh does not renew a timestamp or establish no change. Show material changes to the relevant user, preserve the chosen order and saved snapshots, and require an explicit action to replace a plan or rerun a comparison.
Enquiries and preparation
Story 3.6 owns the contact enquiry list, including context, ordering, deduplication and copying. Epic 2 supplies unresolved conditions. Story 6.5 optionally supplies selected review concerns and does not create another list. Core enquiries work without Epic 6.
Epic 3 helps choose and question a candidate provider. Epic 5 prepares the subsequent pickup, transfer and collection discussion with the usual centre, receiving centre and transport party. It does not record their agreement.

4 Data readiness for delivery
D1 Registration and identity
Existing JKM reference work supplies names, record identifiers, categories, validity terms and coordinates where published [R12]. KPM acquisition is not yet verified.
Required evidence: Branch matching needs corroborating identity evidence. The old import excludes address and contact. Missing or conflicting evidence remains unresolved.
Related requirements: 1.1, 3.1, 3.2
D2 Places and contacts
Existing sourced coordinates may support map locations. OpenStreetMap and Kiddy123 are proposed sources of branch places or public contacts where actually published [R4, R8].
Required evidence: A branch-matched phone, address or contact route needs an inspected source and retrieval record. No inherited JKM phone/address is claimed. Call demonstration requires a verified institutional number.
Related requirements: 1.4, 3.7, 7.2
D3 Service conditions
Kiddy123 is a proposed source of admission ages, care hours, transport and temporary-care statements [R8]. Access and field coverage remain to be established.
Required evidence: Validate real examples for decisive claims before release. A daycare label is not evidence of one-off admission; transport Yes is not pickup coverage. No route-time source or route-time calculation is included.
Related requirements: 1.2, 1.3, Epic 2
